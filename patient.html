01

Blood pressure basics

Hypertension increases the risk of serious cardiovascular complications, making consistent monitoring and proper management important.

  • Record readings consistently so changes can be reviewed over time.
  • Bring repeated high readings to a qualified health professional or your follow-up visit.
  • BPBuddy’s categories support monitoring and do not replace clinical diagnosis.
02

Medication adherence

Long-term hypertension management depends on consistent support and access as well as taking prescribed medicines as directed.

  • Use BPBuddy to record whether prescribed medicine was taken.
  • Do not change or stop medicine based on the website alone.
  • Discuss concerns, side effects, or missed doses with a qualified clinician.
03

Lifestyle self-care

Related hypertension programs in the project literature combine monitoring with health education and lifestyle support.

  • Keep lifestyle notes alongside BP readings to support follow-up conversations.
  • Record physical activity and relevant daily habits consistently.
  • Use professional health guidance for individualized lifestyle recommendations.
04

Follow-up & seeking care

Regular follow-up helps connect monitoring information with professional review and continuing care.

  • Keep scheduled follow-up dates visible and updated.
  • Bring recent BP and medication history to health-worker or clinic review.
  • For severe symptoms or urgent medical concerns, seek appropriate medical care rather than relying on BPBuddy.
