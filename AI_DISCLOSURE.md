## 2. Declaration

- All AI-generated outputs were reviewed, tested, and corrected before inclusion.
- No AI tool was used to fabricate research data, test results, or references.
- All references in the manuscript were independently verified against their DOIs before citation.
- AI tools were not used to produce clinical recommendations, diagnostic logic, or patient-facing medical advice.
- No confidential or sensitive information was uploaded to any AI tool.
- The educational content in `patient.html` was written by the group and is marked in the manuscript as requiring review by qualified health personnel before real-world use.
